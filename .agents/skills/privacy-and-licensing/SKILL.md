# Privacy and Licensing

When adding dependencies, datasets, models, external services, or publishing project outputs:

1. **Privacy:** Never commit credentials, personal information, or sensitive research data.
1. **Data sharing:** Do not send project data to external AI services without explicit authorization.
1. **Licensing:** Check dependencies, models, and datasets for license compatibility with the project.
1. **Dataset publishing:** Never publish or redistribute data from specific third-party datasets without verifying that their licenses or terms explicitly permit it. This includes raw data, samples, subsets, annotations, and transformed or derived data when restrictions apply.
1. **Provenance:** Record the original source, license, and attribution requirements for any dataset used or redistributed.
1. **Attribution:** Preserve copyright notices, license files, and required citations.
1. **Uncertainty:** Flag unclear licenses, privacy risks, or redistribution restrictions for human review. Do not assume that publicly accessible data is openly licensed.
1. **Minimal changes:** Prefer existing dependencies and avoid unnecessary data collection.

Report concerns before making changes. Do not publish restricted or ambiguously licensed data without human approval and verified permission.
